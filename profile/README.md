## Stormwater Intelligence Platform

The Stormwater Intelligence Platform supports professional service delivery by bringing geospatial data, public environmental databases, watershed context, terrain, and weather information into one research environment.

Platform capabilities include:

- Interactive environmental mapping
- Watershed and drainage visualization
- 3D terrain and geospatial analysis
- Weather and precipitation information
- Government environmental-data integration
- Public compliance-data research
- Site-specific environmental context
- Technical workbook and report support

The platform supports research and documentation. Official permits, agency records, site verification, and applicable regulatory requirements remain controlling.
