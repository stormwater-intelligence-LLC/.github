# Ecosystem Registry

Inventory services and accounts Daniel chooses to connect. Include business listings, AI providers, mail and calendars, source control, data and research, owned products, media channels, hosting, and future services. Examples never limit scope. Record stable identifiers, verified owner or account, supported operations, permission scope, connection status, and last verification. Distinguish registered, connected, and operational. An authorized operation at the intended destination establishes operational status for that operation.
